Holdout: Shoot To Survive

A gyroscope-controlled first person shooter for iOS. No virtual joysticks — the device gyroscope is the entire aim system. Physically rotate your device to track enemies approaching from 360°, tap anywhere to shoot.

Built with Ionic React, React Three Fiber, and Capacitor. Published on the App Store by 1608 Studios.

Concept

Most mobile shooters transplant a console joystick onto a touchscreen. Holdout removes that compromise entirely. The gyroscope maps physical device rotation directly to in-game aim — wherever you point your phone, that's where you're looking. Enemies approach from all directions and your body becomes the controller.

Gameplay
Enemies (humanoid figures) spawn and approach from 360°
Rotate your device to face them — the gyroscope controls your aim
Tap anywhere on screen to fire
A radar system tracks all incoming enemies in real time
Don't let enemies reach you — each one that does costs a life
Survive as long as possible and beat your high score

Monetisation

Holdout is free with optional ads:

Rewarded ads — shown when the player runs out of lives and wants to continue. Never forced.
Interstitial ads — shown between rounds at natural break points.

Implemented via the Google Mobile Ads Capacitor plugin.
